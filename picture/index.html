Picture Link Maker

Get a picture onto the board in 3 steps: drag it here, copy the link, paste it in the sheet.
Drag a product photo here
Open the product on any website (TCGplayer, eBay, PSA, the shop...) and drag the picture itself onto this box.
OR
Chase
No picture
Next: in the Google Sheet, click the picture cell of the _CHASE tab (B1 for the main chase) and paste with Cmd+Shift+V (Windows: Ctrl+Shift+V). The board updates in a few seconds.

Using your own photo

  1. Upload the photo to Google Drive.
  2. Right-click it, choose Share, set General access to Anyone with the link, and click Copy link.
  3. Paste that link in the box above to double-check it, then paste it in the sheet.

Good to know

  1. Don't paste the picture itself into the sheet, and don't use =IMAGE(...). The board needs the link as plain text.
  2. If a website won't let its picture show (you'll see a red message here), save the picture and use the Google Drive steps above.
  3. PSA slabs: you can type just the cert number. Once PSA approves our API access the board pulls PSA's own photo. Until then, drag the slab photo from its psacard.com cert page instead.
  4. Rewards: picture in B1, name in B2, prize in B3. For more rewards add rows Chase 2 Image URL, Chase 2 Name, Chase 2 Value (up to 4).
